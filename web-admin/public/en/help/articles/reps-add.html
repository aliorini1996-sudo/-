How do I add a new sales rep and set their login details in Field Sales?

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Open "Sales reps", click "Add rep", enter the name, mobile, username and password, then click "Add rep".

The "Rep management" page is where you create each rep's account and set the username and password they use to sign in to the rep app.

In this tutorial you will open the "Add rep" window, fill in the rep's basic info and login details, then save and copy the login details to hand over to them.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, click "Sales reps" to open the "Rep management" page
  2. Click the "Add rep" button at the top of the page
  3. Under "Basic info", enter the "Name", "Mobile" and "Username"
  4. Enter the "Password" or click "Generate" to create one automatically
  5. Click "Add rep", then copy the login details and hand them to the rep

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • Four fields are required: "Name", "Mobile", "Username" and "Password"; "Email" is optional.
  • The username must be at least 4 characters, and the password at least 8 characters.
  • After saving, the "Rep account created" window shows the login details with a copy button for each line. The password will not be shown again, so copy it before closing the window.
  • If the password is lost later, use the "Reset password" icon in the "Actions" column of the rep's row.
  • In the same window you set the "Status" (Active / Inactive) and the rep's permissions, such as "Sales permissions" and "Customer permissions"; you can change them later from the "Edit" icon.
