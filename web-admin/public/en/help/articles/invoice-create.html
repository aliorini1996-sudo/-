How do I issue a new sales invoice from the dashboard in Field Sales?

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Open "Invoices", click "New invoice", pick the customer and rep, add items, then click "Issue invoice".

The "Invoice management" page lets you issue a sales invoice straight from the office on behalf of one of your reps, as cash, credit or installments, without waiting for the rep in the field.

In this tutorial you will open the "Create new invoice" window, choose the customer, the sales rep and the invoice type, add the items, and then issue the invoice.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, click "Invoices"
  2. Click the "New invoice" button at the top of the page
  3. Choose the "Customer" and the "Sales rep", then set the "Invoice type"
  4. Under "Items", click "Add an item", pick the item, then adjust the "Quantity"
  5. Review the "Grand total", then click "Issue invoice"

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The "Customer" and "Sales rep" fields are required, and an invoice cannot be issued without at least one item; otherwise you will see the message "Choose customer", "Choose rep" or "Add at least one item".
  • The "Date" field is optional: leave it empty and the invoice is dated today.
  • When you choose the "Installments" type, the "Installment schedule" section appears so you can set the "Number of installments", the "First due date" and the "Frequency" (Monthly, Every 15 days, Weekly).
  • You can edit the "Total" on an item row and the unit price updates to match, and you can add a "General discount %" to the whole invoice.
  • After issuing, the message "Invoice created" appears and the invoice document opens right away; you will also find it in the invoices table.
  • The "Invoices" item appears in the sidebar for users who have permission to manage invoices, and the page does not work when accounting is turned off for the company.
