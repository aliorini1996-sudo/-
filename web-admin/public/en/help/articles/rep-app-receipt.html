How does a rep record a receipt from their phone and allocate it to the customer's invoices in Field Sales?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In "Customers" pick the customer, tap "Receipt", enter the amount and payment method, then "Issue receipt".

A receipt records every amount the rep collects from a customer. It reduces the customer's balance and adds the amount to the rep's collection balance.

In this tutorial you will open the customer from the "Customers" tab in the rep app, open the "Issue receipt" screen, enter the amount and allocate it to the open invoices, choose the payment method, then issue the receipt.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the bottom bar, tap "Customers", then "Choose customer"
  2. On the customer card, tap the green "Receipt" button
  3. Enter the "Amount collected", then review "Allocate to invoices"
  4. Pick the "Payment method": "Cash", "Transfer", "Card POS" or "Cheque"
  5. Tap "Issue receipt" at the bottom to save and view the receipt

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The "Collection" tab in the bottom bar lists the receipts you issued, with a date filter; a new receipt is issued from the customer card with the "Receipt" button.
  • The "Collection" tab and the "Receipt" button appear only when accounting is enabled for the company, and the button is disabled if the rep has no permission to issue receipts or the customer is not assigned to them.
  • If the customer has open invoices, allocating to them is required and must be complete before issuing. If there are no open invoices, the receipt is recorded as an advance payment and a credit balance for the customer.
  • Payment method names may differ depending on what the company set on the "Payment methods" page, and added methods such as e-wallets may appear.
  • You can attach up to 4 photos under "Attachments". If the connection drops, the receipt is saved on the device and uploaded automatically once you are back online.
