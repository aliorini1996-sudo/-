How do I read the dashboard in Field Sales to track sales, collections and top reps?

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Open "Dashboard", read "Today's stats", then the sales chart and the "Top reps" list.

The dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. In a single view it brings together today's and this month's sales, collections, customer status and the ranking of your reps.

In this tutorial you will open the dashboard and get to know every card and chart on it, without changing any data.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, click "Dashboard"
  2. Read "Today's stats": "Today's sales", "Today's collection" and the month totals
  3. Review the customer cards: "Active customers" and "Credit limit exceeded"
  4. Follow the "Sales (last 30 days)" chart and hover over it to see each day's sales
  5. Read "Top reps", then "Recent invoices" and "Top customers" at the bottom of the page

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The dashboard is read-only: it has no save or edit button, and its figures refresh automatically every minute.
  • The "Top reps" list shows the first five reps, while "Recent invoices" and "Top customers" show six items each.
  • If the accounting system is turned off for your company, all cards and charts that show amounts are hidden, and only the "Active customers" card remains.
  • The "Dashboard" item in the sidebar appears only for users who have permission to access the dashboard.
  • If you see the message "Could not load dashboard data", click "Retry".
