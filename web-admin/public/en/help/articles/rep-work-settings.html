How do I set a rep's shift time, automatic check-out and route on the map in Field Sales?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In "Sales reps", click "Edit" on the rep, set idle, route and shift options, then click "Save changes".

The "Edit rep" window lets you control each rep's field work settings: when their shift starts and ends, what happens if they stay checked in while idle, and whether their route is drawn on the tracking map.

In this tutorial you will open the rep's window, turn on "Idle check-out reminder, then automatic check-out", set "Show route on map" and "Shift time for alerts", then save.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, click "Sales reps", then the "Edit" icon on the rep's row
  2. Under "Field location restrictions", turn on "Idle check-out reminder, then automatic check-out"
  3. Turn "Show route on map" on or off
  4. Under "Shift time for alerts", set "Shift start", "Shift end" and the days
  5. Click "Save changes" at the bottom of the window

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • These settings are per rep, and the "Sales reps" page is shown to users with the rep management permission.
  • "Idle minutes before the reminder" ranges from 10 to 480 (default 45), and "Response window before automatic check-out (minutes)" from 5 to 120 (default 15). Both fields appear only while the toggle is on.
  • When idle, the rep gets a notification and an "Are you still working?" window. Only "I am still working", real movement of more than 150 m, or issuing a document cancels the automatic check-out; otherwise a check-out is recorded at the last activity time and flagged in the work hours report.
  • Turning off "Show route on map" hides only the drawn path; the current location, visits and punches stay visible, and locations keep being recorded, so the full route returns when you turn it back on.
  • "Shift time for alerts" is optional and used for alerts only: a reminder fifteen minutes before the shift if not punched in, and an alert if the period is still open thirty minutes after it ends. The default days are Sunday to Thursday, and the rep turns alerts on from the punch screen.
  • The same section has other restrictions: "Require location to be on", "Attendance punch at a customer location", "Link punches to one device" and "Random presence checks".
