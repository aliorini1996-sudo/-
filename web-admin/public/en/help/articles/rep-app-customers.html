How do I find a customer and add a new one from the rep app in Field Sales?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In "Customers", search via "Choose customer" or tap "+", add name, mobile, location, "Save customer".

The "Customers" tab in the rep app lists your customers with each one's number, mobile and balance. From here you open any customer's page or add a new customer while you are out in the field.

In this tutorial you will search for a customer by name, mobile or number, add a new customer with a name, mobile and map location, and then take a look at the customer page and what it contains.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the bottom bar, tap "Customers" to see your customer list
  2. Tap "Choose customer" and type the customer's name, mobile or number
  3. Tap the "+" button, then enter the "Customer name" and "Mobile number"
  4. Tap "Capture my current location at the customer", then "Save customer"
  5. Open a customer to see the "Balance", "Account statement" and "Log visit"

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The "+" button for adding a customer only appears for reps who have been given permission to add customers; without it, only the customer list and search are shown.
  • No field in "Add new customer" is required: "Customer name" and "Mobile number" are optional, and if the name is left empty the customer takes the business name or the mobile instead.
  • The map location is optional and shows up for management on the tracking map; capture it at the shop door because it is more accurate than a Google Maps link.
  • If there is no network, the customer is saved on the device and uploaded automatically when the connection returns; the "Customer No." appears after it is uploaded.
  • If management turns on "Duplicate customer prevention" for the rep, saving is refused for a customer with the same mobile, name or location, and the message appears under the duplicated field.
  • On the customer page, "Balance", "Credit limit", "Payment period" and the "New invoice", "Receipt", "Return invoice" and "Account statement" buttons appear when the accounting system is on; the "Statement" entries need permission to view the account statement.
  • The "Edit" button appears for reps allowed to edit customers, and the "Timeline" icon shows everything that happened with the customer, read only.
  • The "Start visit" button at the top of the customer page starts the visit timer, and the visit is recorded automatically when you leave the customer page.
