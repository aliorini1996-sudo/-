How do I set a "Monthly target" for a rep and track their progress in Field Sales?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In "Sales reps", click the "Monthly target" icon, then "Add target", pick type, value and reward, "Save".

The "Monthly target" window lets you set targets for each rep: a sales amount, an item quantity, a percentage of debts collected, a visit count, or new active customers, with a reward earned when the target is achieved.

In this tutorial you will open the targets window from the rep's row, add a target with its value and reward, save it, then follow the progress percentage on the target card.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the sidebar, click "Sales reps" to open "Rep management"
  2. In the "Actions" column, click the rep's "Monthly target" icon
  3. Click "Add target" at the bottom of the window
  4. Under "Target type", pick a type, then enter a value like "Target amount"
  5. Set the "Reward" and its value, then click "Save"

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The "Monthly target" icon appears only when the accounting system is enabled for the company, and requires permission to manage reps.
  • There are five target types: "Sales amount", "Item quantity", "Collect a % of debts", "Visit count" and "New active customers". Sales are counted net of returns.
  • An "Item quantity" target needs a product and its unit; the product list requires product management permission. You can add more products with "Add another product".
  • With "% of salary" the rep sees only the percentage and the salary is not stored in the system. The collection rate is based on the rep's open credit-sale debts at the start of the month.
  • The limit is 20 targets per month, and each target shows on the rep's app home page. Track progress through the progress bar, the percentage, the "Target achieved" badge and the "Targets achieved" counter.
  • You can edit or delete a target from the "Edit target" and "Delete target" icons on its card, and combine two or more targets under one reward with "Combine targets".
