How do I add a new customer in Field Sales from the dashboard?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Open "Customers", click "Add customer", enter the name and mobile number, then click "Add customer" to save.

The "Customer management" page lists all of your company's customers in one table. From there you can add any new customer with their basic details so they appear in lists and invoices.

In this tutorial you open the "Add new customer" dialog, enter the customer name, the mobile number and any other details you need, then save.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. From "Customers", click "Add customer" to open the "Add new customer" dialog.
  2. Under "Basic info", enter the "Customer name" and "Mobile number".
  3. Fill in what you need: "Business name", "Sales channel", "City" and "District".
  4. Click "Add customer" at the bottom of the dialog to save the customer.

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • "Customer name" and "Mobile number" are required (marked with *). If either is left empty, the word "Required" appears under it and the customer is not saved.
  • All other fields are optional: "Business name", "Alt number", "Email", "Commercial register", "Tax number", "Sales channel" and the "Address" section.
  • The "Status" field has three options: "Active", "Inactive" and "Blocked".
  • To show the customer on the tracking map, paste their location link into the "Location link (Google Maps)" field in the "Location on map" section.
  • After saving, the message "Customer added" appears and the dialog closes. The "Cancel" button closes it without saving.
  • The "Customers" item appears in the sidebar only for users who have permission to manage customers.
