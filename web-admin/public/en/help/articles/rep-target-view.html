How does a rep track their targets and reward from the rep app in Field Sales?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

On "Home", the "Monthly target" card shows each target's bar, amount "to go" and reward, plus "Refresh".

When your manager sets targets for you this month, the "Monthly target" card stays on the Home screen of the rep app, so you always know where you stand.

In this tutorial you will read the card: the month and days left, each target's progress bar and percentage, what is left to reach it, the reward line, and how to refresh the figures if they look out of date.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the bottom bar, tap "Home" and scroll to the "Monthly target" card
  2. Each target has a progress bar and percentage; it turns green at "Target achieved"
  3. Below the bar: achieved versus goal, and the amount "to go"
  4. The trophy line shows your reward, such as a "Bonus" or a sales commission

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The card only appears when the manager has set at least one target for the rep this month; with no targets nothing is shown.
  • The card depends on the company's accounting features: if accounting (invoices and collection) is turned off for the company, it does not appear.
  • Targets can be "Monthly sales", a product quantity, collecting a percentage of previous debts, "Visits this month" or new active customers; some have a custom period shown under the title instead of the whole month.
  • A "Combined target" groups several targets under one reward and is "Achieved when all its targets are achieved"; next to it you see how many of its members are done.
  • Figures update automatically after you issue an invoice or after offline invoices sync; the "Refresh" button only appears with the "Figures out of date" notice.
  • Reps can only view their targets; adding or editing a target and its reward is done by the manager in the company dashboard.
  • If "Figures out of date" appears, tap "Refresh" to get the latest figures.
