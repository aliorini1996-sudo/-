How do I issue a sales invoice from the rep's phone in Field Sales?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In "Customers" pick the customer, tap "New invoice", add items, pick the type, then "Review and issue".

Reps issue sales invoices straight from the rep app on their phone at the customer's location, and the invoice then appears in the "Invoices" tab and for management.

In this tutorial you will choose the customer, open "New invoice", add items and quantities, set the payment type, then review and issue the invoice and print or share it.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. In the bottom bar, tap "Customers", then "Choose customer"
  2. On the customer card, tap the "New invoice" button
  3. Pick the "Type": "Cash", "Credit" or "Installments"
  4. Add items from "Choose an item to add" or by tapping an item card
  5. Tap "Review and issue", then "Issue invoice", and print or share

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • Invoice buttons appear only when the accounting system is enabled for the company; "New invoice" stays disabled if the rep lacks permission to issue invoices or to sell in cash or on credit.
  • The "Cash", "Credit" and "Installments" buttons are enabled according to the rep's permissions, and if the price list is cash-only, "Credit" and "Installments" are disabled.
  • The "Invoices" tab in the bottom bar lists issued invoices with a from/to date filter, and tapping one reopens it for printing or sharing; creating an invoice starts from the customer card.
  • You can change quantities on the "Review items" screen before issuing, and go back with the arrow to add more items.
  • When ZATCA Phase 2 is enabled, a tax invoice cannot be issued without an internet connection.
  • If the company enabled signatures, optional "Recipient signature" and "Rep signature" fields appear; if it requires location to be on, the customer card will not open until location is turned on.
