What does the rep app Home screen show in Field Sales, and how do I use it?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Tap "Home" to see your balance, route, "Monthly target", "Today's stats" and "Quick actions".

The "Home" screen is the first thing a rep sees after signing in to the rep app, and it gathers a summary of their day in one place.

In this tour you will get to know the header, the "Your collection balance", "Route" and "Monthly target" cards, the "Today's stats" numbers, then "Quick actions" and the bottom bar with its five tabs.

Video walkthrough

Steps

  1. From the bottom bar, tap "Home" to see a greeting with your name
  2. Check "Your collection balance" and the "Route" card at the top
  3. The "Monthly target" card shows your progress and what is left
  4. Review "Today's stats": "Sales", "Today's collection" and "Invoices"
  5. Under "Quick actions", tap "Attendance" to check in

Illustrated guide

Notes and limits

  • The bottom bar has five tabs: "Home", "Invoices", "Collection", "Customers" and "My stock". If the accounting system is turned off for the company, only "Home" and "Customers" remain.
  • When the accounting system is off, the money cards and "Monthly target" disappear, and "Visits today" and "Customers visited" replace the numbers.
  • "Your collection balance" does not reset daily; it only goes down when the money is handed over to management, and it shows depending on each rep's settings.
  • The "Route" card only shows for reps with a route today, and "Monthly target" only for reps whose manager set targets.
  • "Attendance" only shows if the company has enabled attendance; the "AI rep", "Fuel", "My work number", "Today's report" and "New order" tiles show depending on the enabled features.
  • If the numbers could not be updated, a bar saying the numbers are outdated appears with a "Refresh" button.
