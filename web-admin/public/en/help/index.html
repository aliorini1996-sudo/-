Getting started

0:48

How do I read the dashboard in Field Sales to track sales, collections and top reps?

Open "Dashboard", read "Today's stats", then the sales chart and the "Top reps" list.

Customers and reps

0:36

How do I add a new customer in Field Sales from the dashboard?

Open "Customers", click "Add customer", enter the name and mobile number, then click "Add customer" to save.
0:41

How do I add a new sales rep and set their login details in Field Sales?

Open "Sales reps", click "Add rep", enter the name, mobile, username and password, then click "Add rep".
0:43

How do I track my reps live on the map and view a rep's route for a specific day in Field Sales?

Open "Rep tracking", pick a rep from the list, then choose the day to see their route and visits on the map.

Reps and targets

0:47

How do I change an existing rep's sales, pricing and collection permissions in Field Sales?

Open "Sales reps", click "Edit" on the rep's row, toggle the permissions, then click "Save changes".
0:46

How do I set a rep's shift time, automatic check-out and route on the map in Field Sales?

In "Sales reps", click "Edit" on the rep, set idle, route and shift options, then click "Save changes".
0:50

How do I set a "Monthly target" for a rep and track their progress in Field Sales?

In "Sales reps", click the "Monthly target" icon, then "Add target", pick type, value and reward, "Save".

Rep app

What does the rep app Home screen show in Field Sales, and how do I use it?

Tap "Home" to see your balance, route, "Monthly target", "Today's stats" and "Quick actions".

How do I find a customer and add a new one from the rep app in Field Sales?

In "Customers", search via "Choose customer" or tap "+", add name, mobile, location, "Save customer".

How do I issue a sales invoice from the rep's phone in Field Sales?

In "Customers" pick the customer, tap "New invoice", add items, pick the type, then "Review and issue".

How does a rep record a receipt from their phone and allocate it to the customer's invoices in Field Sales?

In "Customers" pick the customer, tap "Receipt", enter the amount and payment method, then "Issue receipt".

How does a rep track their van stock from their phone and accept a new load in Field Sales?

Tap "My stock" to see each item's "Remaining", request or load goods, accept loads with "Accept and sign".

How does a rep track their targets and reward from the rep app in Field Sales?

On "Home", the "Monthly target" card shows each target's bar, amount "to go" and reward, plus "Refresh".
