Admin dashboard guides

Short video and illustrated guides to everything you need in the Field Sales admin dashboard, step by step.

7 guides

Getting started

0:48

How do I read the dashboard in Field Sales to track sales, collections and top reps?

Open "Dashboard", read "Today's stats", then the sales chart and the "Top reps" list.

Customers and reps

0:36

How do I add a new customer in Field Sales from the dashboard?

Open "Customers", click "Add customer", enter the name and mobile number, then click "Add customer" to save.
0:41

How do I add a new sales rep and set their login details in Field Sales?

Open "Sales reps", click "Add rep", enter the name, mobile, username and password, then click "Add rep".
0:43

How do I track my reps live on the map and view a rep's route for a specific day in Field Sales?

Open "Rep tracking", pick a rep from the list, then choose the day to see their route and visits on the map.

Sales and collections

0:44

How do I issue a new sales invoice from the dashboard in Field Sales?

Open "Invoices", click "New invoice", pick the customer and rep, add items, then click "Issue invoice".
0:38

How do I record a receipt (collection) from a customer in Field Sales?

Open "Receipts", click "New receipt", pick the customer, rep and amount, allocate it, then "Issue receipt".

Products and inventory

0:35

How do I add a new item in Field Sales and set its price and unit?

Open "Products", click "Add item", enter the code, name, unit of measure and base price, then click "Add item".
