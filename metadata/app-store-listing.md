## English (en-US)

**App Name (≤30):**
`FieldSales — Sales Rep`

**Subtitle (≤30):**
`Field invoicing & collections`

**Promotional Text (≤170):**
`Issue ZATCA tax invoices and receipts, collect payments, and manage customers and van stock from the field — works even offline.`

**Keywords (≤100):**
`sales rep,field sales,invoice,zatca,receipt,collections,customers,van sales,distribution,statement,stock`

**Description:**
```
FieldSales — Sales Rep is the field tool for sales and distribution teams. Built for the rep in the market: issue ZATCA-compliant tax invoices, record collections, track customer balances and van stock — with a fast, simple Arabic-first interface.

Features:
• Issue ZATCA-compliant tax invoices with QR code; share as PDF instantly.
• Payment receipts and debt collection with a clear running collection balance.
• Per-customer account statements with running balance, credit limit and over-limit alerts.
• Sales return invoices in one tap.
• Customer management and on-the-spot customer creation (per rep permission).
• Van inventory: log what you loaded; it decrements with every sale.
• Product catalog with tax-inclusive pricing.
• Offline mode: create invoices and receipts offline; they sync automatically when back online.
• Field visit location tracking (enabled by the company admin, with rep consent).

This app is an internal business tool for employees of distribution and sales companies. It provides no consumer financial service or payments. Requires an account issued by your company on the FieldSales platform.

Learn more: https://fieldsa.net
```
